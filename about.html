About This Fan Project

Hogwarts: Houses & Wizards is a handcrafted fan site built to celebrate the lore of the wizarding world through accessible, data-rich storytelling.

Project Purpose

This interactive guide brings together canonical Hogwarts houses and celebrated witches and wizards. It distills data into beautiful, responsive layouts, helping fans and newcomers alike remember the key traits, artifacts, and milestones that define the magical world.
